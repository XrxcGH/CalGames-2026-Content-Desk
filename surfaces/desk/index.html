Cameras ·

The shot, not the graphic. Screens follow the match on their own, so a late cut leaves the graphics right. Autopilot never cuts away from the field mid-match; you always can. Arm the automatic cuts in Show automation below.

Show automation ·

A cue is the desk doing one thing by itself: cutting to the field when the match arms, ducking the music when the announcer talks. Every cue starts off, and an off cue still counts what it would have done, so you can watch it be right before you trust it. Run now fires one by hand whatever its setting.
